BEYOND PROJECTS

Volunteering & Tech Events

AWS Community Day Manila 2026

Volunteering for one of the country’s biggest cloud communities was my first real taste of the tech outside school. I worked on the official event page and certificate generator with a co-designer, but getting to usher, handle sponsors, and facilitate workshops on the actual event days was what made it special. I really enjoyed connecting with people and learning on the ground.

AWS Beyond the Build

After having such a great time at AWS Community Day, I knew I wanted to keep showing up. I volunteered for one of their monthly meetups—a collab between AWSUG and Tutorials Dojo. My role was pretty small, but the day meant a lot to me. I got to talk and connect with so many people in the community, share stories, and even stumbled into opportunities I never really expected to come my way.

AWS Build Over Nights: Kiro x QuickHackathon

My third time volunteering for AWSUG! This time around, I was part of the relations team helping out with sponsors and partners. It was such a rewarding run because not only did I get to see firsthand that the sponsor I was handling had a great experience, but I also got to spend the whole event doing it alongside my friends.
